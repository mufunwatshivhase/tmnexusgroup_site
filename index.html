    About TM Nexus Group

    Where technology meets the business behind it.

    TM Nexus Group started from a simple observation. Small and growing businesses in South Africa need more than a good website. They also need a registered company, a valid tax clearance, UIF and COIDA in order, and a business profile that wins tenders. Usually those come from five different people.

    We are a nexus: the point where those needs connect. Our founder is a software engineer, so the technology side is built properly, with clean code, secure hosting and automated deployments. Our business support team handles the compliance work that keeps your company eligible for contracts, funding and bank accounts.

    The result is one partner who understands both your systems and your paperwork.

    Mission

    Give every business access to enterprise‑grade technology and hassle‑free compliance, at a price that makes sense for a small company.

    Vision

    To be South Africa's most trusted partner for businesses building their future.

    What makes us different

    Built by an engineer. Run like a business.

    Engineering first

    Your site and software are built with modern frameworks, version control and CI/CD, so they're easy to grow later.

    Compliance included

    We understand CIPC, SARS and Department of Labour requirements, and we keep your records current.

    Plain language

    Clear quotes, clear timelines, and explanations without jargon. You always know what you're paying for.

    Privacy Policy
    
    
    Last updated: 13 October 2026
    
    
    TM Nexus Group respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).
    
    
    What we collect
    
    
    When you contact us we collect your name, email, phone number, company name and the details you share about your project. For compliance services we collect the documents and identity information needed to file with CIPC, SARS or the Department of Employment and Labour.
    
    
    How we use it
    
    
    Only to respond to your enquiry, deliver the services you've engaged us for, and meet our own legal obligations. We do not sell your information.
    
    
    Sharing
    
    
    We share information with government bodies and service providers only when required to complete your service, such as filing a CIPC annual return or registering for VAT.
    
    
    Your rights
    
    
    You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@tmnexusgroup.co.za.
